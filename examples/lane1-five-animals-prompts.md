# Five-animal Lane 1 image prompts

These reference images were generated individually with ChatGPT image
generation. No image-to-3D or mesh generator was used.

Every prompt used this shared direction:

> Create a clean Minecraft mob concept image on a plain warm-white studio
> background. Show the full creature in a neutral three-quarter view, facing
> slightly right. Use only coherent axis-aligned cuboid anatomy, crisp
> square-pixel textures, no smooth curves, no voxel noise, no scenery, no text,
> and no UI. Keep all four limbs and important appendages visibly separated.
> Make it look like a polished vanilla-compatible Minecraft creature reference
> suitable for reconstruction as a Blockbench cuboid model.

Animal-specific direction:

- **Platypus:** Low brown body, broad blue-gray duck bill, four dark webbed
  feet, tiny side eyes, and a broad flat dark tail.
- **Chimpanzee:** Black-brown fur, long weight-bearing arms, compact bent hind
  legs, tan face and ears, broad hands and feet, and no tail.
- **Elephant:** Massive gray body, four pillar legs, broad frontal ears, long
  segmented trunk, paired ivory tusks, and a short tufted tail.
- **Tiger:** Orange coat with bold black stripes, white muzzle and underside,
  strong paws, small ears, and a long dark-tipped striped tail.
- **Coyote:** Lean gray-brown body, oversized upright ears, narrow cream muzzle,
  long tan legs, dark paws, and a low bushy dark-tipped tail.
